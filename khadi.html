Known by most. Chosen by few.

What this means for Khadi

  1. Sell quality and comfort, not heritage. Buyers motivated by quality prefer Khadi far more than those buying for tradition, and fans stand out most on Khadi being a cool fabric for India's heat.
  2. Get Khadi into people's hands. Buyers are much more likely to recommend it than non-buyers, and lack of awareness and availability block many who have never tried it. Sampling, retail presence and online availability matter more than more brand advertising.
  3. Lead with home décor, then accessories. Home décor draws more interest than clothing, and accessories come close behind; both avoid the "kurta only" image.
  4. Win over 26 to 35 year olds with modern styles. They are the least convinced age group and the core of the working-age market.
About the data.
  • Online questionnaire covering demographics, awareness, buying behaviour and 15 attitude statements on a 5-point scale, shared through personal and social networks. A convenience sample, so groups are not represented in proportion to India's population.
  • "Prefer Khadi" means answering Yes to "Do you prefer clothing made from Khadi fabric?". Agreement means Agree or Strongly agree. Percentages are rounded.
  • The original 2023 SPSS work covered frequencies only; the comparisons between groups here are new. Only summary figures are published on this page, not individual responses.
