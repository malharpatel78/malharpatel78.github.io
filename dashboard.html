How to read this
  • Source: Meta Ads Manager export, results counted as lead-form leads (7-day click or 1-day view attribution). Campaigns before Oct 2023 are not in Meta's export window.
  • Campaign type is my grouping, based on keywords in campaign names. Campaigns I could not classify are shown as "Other / unlabeled".
  • Reach (1.64M) is Meta's de-duplicated total. Adding campaign-level reach would count the same person many times, so it is only shown in total.
  • The export covers the ad account across two businesses: Uniq Synergy (to Aug 2024) and local recruitment for my family's other business.
  • Cost is in Indian rupees (₹). At about ₹83 to the dollar, ₹16 is roughly 19 cents.
